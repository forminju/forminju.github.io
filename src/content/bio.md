I'm **MinJu Jeon**, an AI researcher working on **data-centric training for multimodal and language models**. I'm currently a research intern at **LG AI Research (EXAONE Lab)**.

My work explores how data curation, synthetic data generation, retrieval augmentation, and training-time data design shape what a model can do and how reliably it does it. In practice that spans multimodal understanding, dense video captioning, text-video retrieval, multilingual language and speech modeling, and agentic visual-code environments.

Before joining LG, I worked on multilingual speech (grapheme-to-phoneme, robust TTS) at **NAVER Cloud** Voice Tech, and on vision-language understanding at **Hanyang University**'s Multimodal AI Lab, advised by [Prof. Dong-Jin Kim](https://sites.google.com/view/hyu-mm/home), where I completed my **M.S. in Data Science** in August 2026.
